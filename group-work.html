Reflection: Working in a Group

Framework: Tuckman's stages of group development

Write this yourself (delete this box when done). This is worth 15 marks and the top band needs deep, critical reflection, so be honest and specific: name real moments, not generic statements. Answer the guide questions under each stage.

Introduction

[How was the group formed? How many members? What was the task?]

Forming

[Did you know your group mates beforehand? First impressions, how roles and tasks started to be discussed.]

Storming

[Any disagreements or challenges? How were they resolved? If there was little conflict, explain why and what that meant.]

Norming

[How did the group develop shared expectations or routines: meeting times, WhatsApp group rules, task split?]

Performing

[Did the group function effectively? Describe a moment where it clicked, such as preparing or delivering the presentation.]

Adjourning

[How did the group conclude? Was there closure or unfinished tension?]

What I Learned About Communication and Teamwork

[2-3 concrete lessons, and how you will apply them in future group work or in the workplace.]
