Application Letter: Industrial Attachment

Template letter. Tailor the bracketed parts for each organisation. Do not include your home address or ID number.

Gaborone, Botswana
[Professional email address]
[Date]

The Human Resources Manager
[Company name]
[Company address or city]

Dear Sir/Madam,

RE: APPLICATION FOR INDUSTRIAL ATTACHMENT - BSc COMPUTER SYSTEMS ENGINEERING

I am a first-year BSc Computer Systems Engineering student at Botswana Accountancy College, and I am writing to apply for an industrial attachment at [Company name] in [department, e.g. Network Operations / IT Support].

I hold CompTIA A+ and Cisco Networking Academy certifications and am currently preparing for my CCNA. Through my part-time work in IT procurement and vendor partnerships, I have gained practical exposure to enterprise networking and infrastructure solutions and to professional vendor communication. These experiences have strengthened my technical foundation and my ability to work in a deadline-driven environment.

I am particularly drawn to [Company name] because [one specific, researched reason: a project, a technology, or the company's reputation]. I am confident that I can contribute through [two relevant strengths, e.g. troubleshooting ability and clear documentation], while learning from your team.

I am available for attachment from [start date] to [end date]. My CV is attached for your consideration, and I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application at an interview.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Yours faithfully,

Kgosi [Surname]
BSc Computer Systems Engineering
Botswana Accountancy College
