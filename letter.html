Application Letter: Industrial Attachment

An application letter for an industrial attachment, which I adapt for each organisation I apply to.

Gaborone, Botswana
19 November 2026

The Human Resources Manager
Botswana Fibre Networks (BoFiNet)
Gaborone

Dear Sir/Madam,

RE: APPLICATION FOR INDUSTRIAL ATTACHMENT - BSc COMPUTER SYSTEMS ENGINEERING

I am a first-year BSc Computer Systems Engineering student at Botswana Accountancy College, and I am writing to apply for an industrial attachment at Botswana Fibre Networks (BoFiNet) in your Network Operations department.

I have completed Cisco Networking Academy courses and Fortinet NSE 1 to 3 in network security, and I am currently preparing for my CCNA. Through my part-time work in IT procurement and vendor partnerships, I have gained practical exposure to enterprise networking and infrastructure solutions and to professional vendor communication. These experiences have strengthened my technical foundation and my ability to work in a deadline-driven environment.

I am particularly drawn to BoFiNet because it runs Botswana's national fibre backbone, the infrastructure that connects the country's service providers to each other and to the world. Learning how a network at that scale is operated and kept secure is exactly the experience I want. I am confident that I can contribute through my networking foundation and my experience communicating professionally with vendors, while learning from your team.

I am available for attachment during the period set by Botswana Accountancy College. My CV is attached for your consideration, and I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application at an interview.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Yours faithfully,

Kgosi Blanda
BSc Computer Systems Engineering
Botswana Accountancy College
