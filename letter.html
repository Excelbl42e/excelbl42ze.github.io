My letter applying for an industrial attachment. I change the company details for each place I apply to.

Gaborone, Botswana
19 November 2026

The Human Resources Manager
Botswana Fibre Networks (BoFiNet)
Gaborone

Dear Sir/Madam,

RE: APPLICATION FOR INDUSTRIAL ATTACHMENT, BSc COMPUTER SYSTEMS ENGINEERING

I am a first-year BSc Computer Systems Engineering student at Botswana Accountancy College, and I would like to apply for an industrial attachment in your Network Operations department.

I have completed Cisco Networking Academy courses and Fortinet NSE 1 to 3 in network security, and I am currently studying for my CCNA. I also work part-time in IT procurement and vendor partnerships, where I deal with enterprise networking and infrastructure products and communicate with vendors professionally. This work has taught me to meet deadlines and to communicate clearly.

I am applying to BoFiNet because you run Botswana's national fibre backbone, which connects the country's service providers to each other and to the rest of the world. I want to learn how a network of that size is run and kept secure. In return I can offer my networking foundation, my certifications and my experience working with vendors.

I am available for attachment during the period set by Botswana Accountancy College. My CV is attached, and I would welcome the chance to discuss my application in an interview.

Thank you for your time.

Yours faithfully,

Kgosi Blanda
BSc Computer Systems Engineering
Botswana Accountancy College
