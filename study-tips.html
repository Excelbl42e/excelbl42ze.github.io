Study Tips and Resources

Strategies that work for a student balancing studies and work.

Pomodoro technique

25 minutes focused, 5 minutes break. After four rounds, take a longer break.

Active recall

Close the notes and write down what you remember. Use flashcards for terms and commands.

Hands-on labs

For networking, practise in Cisco Packet Tracer instead of only reading.

Weekly planning

Block out class, work and study time every Sunday and review it each week.

Useful resources

  • Cisco Networking Academy
  • Professor Messer (CompTIA videos)
  • Khan Academy
