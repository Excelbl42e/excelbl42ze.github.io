Study tips that work for me

I balance university, part-time work and my own projects, and I have ADHD, so these are the methods that actually work for me.

Break every task into objectives

Before I start anything, I write down what "done" looks like as a short list and tick things off. It stops me drifting and makes sure the work is done properly.

The 80/20 rule

Most of the marks come from a small number of topics. I find those first, get them solid, and then test myself on every way they can be asked.

Pomodoro

I work in short focused blocks, around 25 minutes, with a 5-minute break between them. Long sessions wear me out and I stop taking anything in.

Learn where ideas come from

For maths and engineering I look up how a topic was discovered and what problem it solved. It makes the topic make sense, so I can work it out again instead of cramming it.

Quiz yourself

I make a short booklet from the marking scheme, split into sections, then quiz myself on it. Testing myself shows me what I actually know.

Practise, don't just read

For networking I build things in Cisco Packet Tracer instead of only reading the notes.

Plan with slack time

Things always take longer than I think, so I leave space in my plans for that.

Cut distractions

I took YouTube off my home screen and I don't use TikTok. When I'm bored now, I read.

Resources I use

  • Cisco Networking Academy: free networking and cybersecurity courses
  • Fortinet Training Institute: free security courses (where I did NSE 1-3)
  • Professor Messer: free IT certification videos
  • Khan Academy: maths
