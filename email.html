Email Template

A short covering email to send with my CV and application letter.

To: [recipient email]
Subject: Industrial Attachment Application - Kgosi [Surname], BSc Computer Systems Engineering

Dear [Mr/Ms Surname / Hiring Manager],

I am a first-year Computer Systems Engineering student at Botswana Accountancy College, and I am applying for an industrial attachment with [Company name].

Please find attached my CV and application letter. In brief, I hold CompTIA A+ and Cisco NetAcad certifications, am working towards my CCNA, and have practical experience in IT procurement and vendor communication.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I can contribute to your team. I am available at [phone number] or by email at your convenience.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Kind regards,
Kgosi [Surname]
BSc Computer Systems Engineering | Botswana Accountancy College
[Phone] | [Professional email]

Attachments: Kgosi_Surname_CV.pdf, Kgosi_Surname_Application_Letter.pdf

Why this works

  • Specific subject line so it is not overlooked.
  • Short body: who I am, what I attach, what I offer, what I ask.
  • Professional tone and clear attachment names.
