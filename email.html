Email

The short email I would send with my CV and application letter.

To: hr@bofinet.co.bw (example)
Subject: Industrial Attachment Application: Kgosi Blanda, BSc Computer Systems Engineering

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am a first-year Computer Systems Engineering student at Botswana Accountancy College, and I am applying for an industrial attachment with Botswana Fibre Networks (BoFiNet).

I have attached my CV and application letter. In short, I have completed Cisco NetAcad courses and Fortinet NSE 1 to 3, I am studying for my CCNA, and I have work experience in IT procurement and vendor communication.

I would be glad to discuss my application further. You can reach me by email at any time.

Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,
Kgosi Blanda
BSc Computer Systems Engineering, Botswana Accountancy College

Attachments: Kgosi_Blanda_CV.pdf, Kgosi_Blanda_Application_Letter.pdf

Why I wrote it this way

  • The subject line says exactly what the email is and who it's from, so it doesn't get missed.
  • I kept it short: who I am, what I've attached, what I can offer and what I'm asking for. The details are in the CV and letter.
  • I kept the tone formal and named the attachments clearly, so it's easy to see what's included.
