Email Template

A short covering email to send with my CV and application letter.

To: hr@bofinet.co.bw (example)
Subject: Industrial Attachment Application - Kgosi Blanda, BSc Computer Systems Engineering

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am a first-year Computer Systems Engineering student at Botswana Accountancy College, and I am applying for an industrial attachment with Botswana Fibre Networks (BoFiNet).

Please find attached my CV and application letter. In brief, I have completed Cisco NetAcad courses and Fortinet NSE 1 to 3, am working towards my CCNA, and have practical experience in IT procurement and vendor communication.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I can contribute to your team. I am available by email at your convenience.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Kind regards,
Kgosi Blanda
BSc Computer Systems Engineering | Botswana Accountancy College

Attachments: Kgosi_Blanda_CV.pdf, Kgosi_Blanda_Application_Letter.pdf

Why this works

  • Specific subject line so it is not overlooked.
  • Short body: who I am, what I attach, what I offer, what I ask.
  • Professional tone and clear attachment names.
