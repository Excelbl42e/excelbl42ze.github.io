About me

I've been working part-time in IT procurement and vendor partnerships since I was 17. Most of my day-to-day there is tenders and dealing with vendors like Dell, Cisco and Object First, so I got to see enterprise IT before I even started university.

I'm an optimist. Things don't always go my way. I studied really hard for my Form 5 exams and still finished with 43 points, mostly because of my ADHD. But whatever goes wrong, at school, at work or here at BAC, I get back up and keep going.

I want to make an honest living in IT and engineering. The area I'm most interested in for my career is FPGAs, which are chips you can reprogram into your own custom hardware. I'm already building my own cybersecurity and game projects, and I'd like to turn them into companies one day. In my free time I build simple circuit boards and make games with the help of AI.
