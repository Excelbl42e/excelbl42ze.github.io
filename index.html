About Me

I am a first-year BSc Computer Systems Engineering student at Botswana Accountancy College (BAC) in Gaborone, Botswana. I am passionate about how technology infrastructure works, from enterprise networks to the software that runs on top of them.

Alongside my studies I have worked in IT procurement and vendor partnerships since I was 17, which has given me early exposure to enterprise hardware, networking and data protection solutions from vendors such as Dell, Cisco and Object First.

I am driven by optimism for a better future. Things do not always end in my favour: I studied very hard for my Form 5 exams and finished with 43 points, largely because of my ADHD and neurodivergence. But no matter how many setbacks I face, at school, at work or at university, I always get back up.

I want to make an honest living in IT and engineering, and my biggest career interest is FPGAs (chips that can be reconfigured into custom hardware). I am already working on my own game and cybersecurity projects, which I hope to turn into companies one day. In my free time I enjoy electronics projects, like building beginner circuit boards, and developing games with the help of AI.
